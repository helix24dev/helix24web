Google Ads vs Meta Ads

Google Ads vs Meta Ads: Which Works Better for Kerala Businesses?

One of the most common questions business owners in Kerala ask is: "Should I invest in Google Ads PPC or Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)?" The short answer depends on search intent.

1. Google Ads (Search & Performance Max)

Google Ads targets prospective customers actively searching for terms like "best clinic in Kochi" or "web development company Kozhikode". Search traffic carries high purchase intent, leading to immediate conversions, though Cost-Per-Click (CPC) can be higher.

2. Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)

Meta Ads excel at visual storytelling, impulse discovery, and broad local reach. For real estate, fashion, healthcare, restaurants, and D2C brands in Kerala, Meta Ads generate large lead volumes at significantly lower cost per engagement.

The Winning Approach: Combine Google Ads for high-intent capture with Meta Ads for retargeting and brand awareness.

Local SEO & Google Maps

Local SEO for Businesses in Kochi & Kozhikode: A Complete Guide

When prospective customers search for services on Google, over 60% click on the Google Local 3-Pack Map Results. Dominating local search in Kochi, Ernakulam, or Kozhikode requires a dedicated Local SEO framework:

Key Local SEO Pillars

  • Google Business Profile (GBP) Optimization: Maintain identical Name, Address, and Phone (NAP) details, upload geotagged images, and regularly collect 5-star customer reviews.
  • Localized Keyword Strategy: Incorporate high-intent location terms like "Meta ads agency Kochi" or "web design Kozhikode" naturally across your page headers and meta tags.
  • Schema Structured Data: Implement LocalBusiness JSON-LD schema so search engines index your service area accurately.
